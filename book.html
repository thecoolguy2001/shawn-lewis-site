About the book.

You were a spiritual being long before you were given a name. In this short, direct guide, Shawn Lewis shows why your spirit, not your body, is who you really are, and how living from that truth changes everything.

Drawing on more than a decade of spiritual study, Shawn walks you through spiritual awakening and alignment, the battle between spirit and flesh, the power of manifestation, why thoughts are energy, the universal laws that shape your life, and how to step outside the matrix and unlock the spiritual power already within you.

For anyone who feels there is more to life than what they can see and touch, this is your invitation to know who you are, and live who you are.
