About the Author

Shawn Lewis.

Spiritual motivational speaker and author.

His story.

Shawn Lewis is a spiritual motivational speaker and author, and the voice behind Your Body Doesn’t Have a Spirit, Your Spirit Has a Body, a book that is part of his larger mission to help others elevate spiritually and connect with God consciously.

Raised in a Christian household by two Christian parents, Shawn followed the faith of his family. As a teenager on the streets of California, he was found by a pastor who invited him into The Eagles Nest Christian Center and took him under his wing. For six years, Shawn was mentored by his pastor, attending church and studying the Bible side by side with him.

During those years, Shawn began to notice scriptures that seemed to contradict one another. He brought his questions to his pastor again and again, but the answers never connected with his spirit. Unwilling to settle for answers that didn’t ring true, he began to look beyond Christianity to see whether the same contradictions existed elsewhere. His search carried him through Islam and Egyptology, and eventually into the study of consciousness and spirituality.

That study opened an inner door. Shawn’s spirit was filled with God consciousness and a deeper spiritual inner-standing. Twenty years ago, he stopped subscribing to any one religion and committed himself to learning and practicing spirituality, which he found to be the truest way to connect with God.

Today, Shawn shares what he has learned with readers who are searching, questioning, or ready to go deeper. His message is simple: you are not a body that happens to have a spirit. You are a spirit having a human experience in a body, and when you understand that, a conscious connection with God becomes possible.
